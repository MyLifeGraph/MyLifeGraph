GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Is this the real app?

This page contains a simplified, scripted demo with invented data. The real MyLifeGraph app is a student-built pilot for Android and the web. Open the app to create your own account.

What works on Android and the web?

Both offer check-ins, planning, insights and the Coach. Health Connect, app blocking and on-device speech are Android features. Permissions, downloaded models and compatible devices may be required.

Who can see my data?

This demo never connects to an account or database. In the real app, account data is stored in the cloud. Coach requests share relevant context with your selected provider. Health import and push notifications are optional. Review the app’s privacy information before using it.

Does it automatically change my plans?

No. Planning suggestions stay previews until you confirm them. The Coach helps you understand your recorded data; it does not silently edit your calendar. Patterns show associations, not proof of cause.

Can I use it in German?

This website is available in English and German. In the app, Coach conversations and the Ultra Quick Check-in voice guide support both languages. The rest of the app interface is currently English.
