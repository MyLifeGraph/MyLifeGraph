# Local website identifiers

The six brand paths in `blocking_website_marks.dart` are unmodified geometry
from Simple Icons **11.15.0**, used as local website identifiers. Color/padding
is presentation only. These are brand glyphs, not claims of current downloaded
favicons or installed Android launcher artwork. Unknown hosts use a neutral globe;
lookalike domains never inherit a trusted brand mark. Runtime rendering sends no
domains to a website, favicon proxy or other provider.

Source: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons>

- `instagram.svg`, `youtube.svg`, `x.svg`, `facebook.svg`, `reddit.svg`, `twitch.svg`
- CC0: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md>
- Trademark rights are not granted by CC0. Marks identify their respective sites;
  no affiliation, endorsement or security guarantee is implied.

App icons are separate: Android PackageManager supplies the actual installed
app drawable as PNG bytes. Never substitute website brand glyphs for those bytes.
