# Space backdrop assets

These two local WebP files are the responsive, presentation-only backdrop for
the Space appearance. They were generated with the built-in Imagegen tool and
then mechanically converted to WebP. The app adds its own deterministic,
reduced-motion-aware star overlay; the raster images contain no UI or text.

## Landscape prompt

> Create a photorealistic deep-space astrophotography background for a premium
> productivity app, wide landscape composition. It must look like an authentic
> long-exposure telescope capture: a restrained deep star field, irregular dark
> molecular dust lanes, and faint emission-nebula structure in physically
> plausible cyan, blue-violet, and very subtle magenta. Keep a broad, quiet,
> near-black navy center as negative space for readable interface content, with
> richer luminous detail toward the upper right and lower left. Stars must be
> tiny, varied, naturally colored pinpoints; only a very few bright stars may
> show subtle optical diffraction. Use fine photographic grain, deep dynamic
> range, full-bleed framing, and observational realism. No planet, moon,
> spacecraft, rings, constellation lines, orbit graphics, ribbons, text, logo,
> border, UI, cartoon, painting, fantasy-book illustration, neon vaporwave,
> artificial graphic gradient, or lens flare.

## Portrait prompt

> Using the selected landscape deep-field image as the visual reference, create
> a matching 9:16 portrait sister image of the same plausible celestial region.
> Preserve its authentic telescope-photo character, dark molecular clouds,
> restrained cyan/violet emission, natural star distribution, grain, and deep
> exposure. Recompose rather than simply crop: reserve a calm, dark vertical
> center for mobile UI, place the most detailed luminous structure toward the
> upper right and lower left, and keep all edges suitable for full-bleed cover.
> No new celestial object, planet, moon, spacecraft, rings, constellation
> drawing, graphic ribbon, text, logo, UI, illustration, or synthetic neon
> effect.

The committed outputs are `space-deep-field-landscape.webp` (1448×1086) and
`space-deep-field-portrait.webp` (941×1672).
